# 1. Product Overview

## 1.1 Product Introduction

### MindPalace Odin1 Lite：Compact Spatial-Memory Module

> **Born for robots, drones and cutting-edge intelligent devices**

By deeply fusing LiDAR and vision sensors, Odin1 Lite achieves high-precision, wide-angle real-time 3D perception. With the built-in MindSLAM<sup>TM</sup> high-performance fusion SLAM algorithm, it delivers persistent spatial memory. It is suitable for rapid integration and deployment on size- and cost-sensitive robots, autonomous navigation platforms and intelligent edge devices, and can operate stably in complex indoor and outdoor environments.
