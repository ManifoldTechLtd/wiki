# 4. Power and Connection

## 4.1 External Power System Design Guide

1. **Voltage range requirements**

   Odin1 Lite's nominal operating voltage is **DC 9–24 V**. It is recommended to use a **12 V / 2 A** DC adapter (≥ 24 W power margin); otherwise, the device may fail to start or work unstably due to insufficient instantaneous current.

2. **Power adapter notes**

   - Connect the adapter through the **DC jack** of the power-and-data splitter aviation cable; the DC jack specification is **5.5×2.1 mm**.
   - Odin1 Lite **does not include a power adapter** by default; users must prepare a compliant 12 V adapter.

3. **Voltage surge protection warning**

   ⚠️ Voltage fluctuations exceeding the rated upper limit on the power line may cause permanent damage to the device. It is recommended to add overvoltage protection circuitry in the power loop.

4. **Power consumption characteristics**

   | Operating State | Power Characteristics |
   |:-:|:-:|
   | Stable operation | Typical ≈ **11** W |
   | Cold-start peak | Maximum **24** W |

5. **Key power selection parameters**

   - Continuous output capability ≥ **2 A**
   - Recommended power margin ≥ **24 W**
   - Recommended overvoltage protection threshold ≤ **26 V**

## 4.2 Connection Steps

Odin1 Lite communicates with the PC via Ethernet.

1. Insert the aviation plug of the **power-and-data splitter aviation cable** into the Odin1 Lite interface in the correct groove direction, and tighten the threaded ring.
2. Connect the **DC jack** to the 12 V / 2 A adapter and power on; the device indicator staying on means power-on success.
3. Connect the **RJ45 network cable** to the PC Ethernet port (or switch).
4. Configure the PC Ethernet card IP to `192.168.1.x/24` (same subnet as the device `192.168.1.251`).
5. After confirming communication is normal with `ping 192.168.1.251`, you can start the driver or access the HTTP Dashboard.

> 🔧 For detailed network configuration steps, see [7. Odin1 Lite Quick Start (Ubuntu) §7.2.3 Network Configuration](./7.%20Odin1Lite%20Quick%20start%20for%20ubuntu.html#723-network-configuration).
